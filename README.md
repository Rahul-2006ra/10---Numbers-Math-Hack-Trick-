📘 10-Number Math Hack
A simple and fast trick to add 10 consecutive numbers without doing step-by-step addition.

🔍 The Trick :-
Sum = (Average of the Middle Two Numbers) × 10

Works for any 10 consecutive numbers — quick, clean, and time-saving.
